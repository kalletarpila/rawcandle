## Provider Ownership Formula

For each row, native price, marketcap, pb, sharesbas, sharefactor and equityusd come from the **same accepted ARQ observation**, identified by company_id, observation_id and content_hash. No cross-row division, EPS weighted-average substitution, score/history fallback or current-price substitution was used.

```
N_cap = marketcap / provider_price
N_pb = provider_pb * equityusd / provider_price
N_factor = sharesbas * sharefactor
cap residual % = abs(N_factor / N_cap - 1) * 100
PB residual % = abs(N_pb / N_cap - 1) * 100
```

Sharadar documents marketcap as price × basic shares × sharefactor, and sharefactor as a unit adjustment, including depositary units and some class conventions. Its definitions do not independently guarantee that every primary-class price equals the price of every economic class. Basic shares are a point share count, distinct from EPS weighted-average shares. [Sharadar fundamentals definitions](https://sharadar.com/docs/fundamentals), [Sharadar daily definitions](https://sharadar.com/docs/daily).

For BA, DIS, GM, GE, RTX, T, DHR, BSX, BMY, AMT, O, PSA, OXY, TMUS, VZ and PNR, sharesbas matches the cited ordinary/common cover count, not EPS average or a separately traded preferred issue. Exact count equality plus provider cap reconstruction supports the entire common-share base at that date. Preferred claims remain outside this numerator: the unchanged denominator is **parent equity with preferred exclusion unproven**, not certified common-only book value. All proposed releases preserve that reporting-only limitation.

The F, CMCSA, TSN, HVT, HEI, KELYA, WSO, BIO and HOV arithmetic shows aggregate class counts, not merely ticker-class counts. For example HEI uses 139,757,405 aggregate shares, whereas the quoted HEI class has 55,241,647. The provider numerator is therefore a primary-price proxy across both classes, not the sum of their separately observed market values. CHTR demonstrates the opposite kind of case: sharesbas matches quoted A alone, requiring verification of the other class's economic rights and LLC ownership. UHAL's two listings were established, but its count decomposition was not independently completed because the filing reader failed; it stays unresolved.

Five concepts must remain distinct: legal classes; separately traded classes; one quoted plus unlisted voting classes; equal cash-dividend examples; materially different economic rights. Equal dividends on one declaration do not prove equal liquidation rights, conversion rights or contemporaneous prices. TSN/HVT/HOV/BIO explicitly prevent a universal equal-unit assumption. CWEN's converted listing structure still does not align all legal shares with parent-only equity. PCG's matching count includes subsidiary-held parent shares. Formula equality cannot settle any of these perimeter problems.

## Current-Price Carry-Forward Risk

Provider dates in the ownership cohort are **29–149 days** before as-of (median **62 days**). Availability age passing the existing 180-day gate does not prove static shares. Cover share-count date can precede filing date; June GE/VZ counts are examples. Current quote is dated October 6. Proposed P/B must display both price date and share-count/source date, describe carried shares as an approximation, and invalidate established intervening changes without a reconciled newer count.

The bounded stored corporate-action query between each provider date and October 7 returned BIAF and BRCC splits on August 24, SEGG split August 3, and QXO relation to preferred QXO-PB August 28. QXO's relation to preferred does not itself prove a common-share count change. SEGG also fails the independent historical price witness. GPMT/LEXX/NRDY fail that witness without independently resolved causes. These four would remain invalid after an ownership-only relaxation.

## Recommended Ownership Contract

Propose a separate next implementation with review records bound to **company_id + security_id/issuer + observation_id/content_hash + validity interval**, evidence URL and units. Do not implement ticker lists from this audit.

1. Define **whole parent common economic units**, excluding treasury and subsidiary/LLC noncontrolling units, distinct from float and depositary certificates. Require the same parent perimeter as equityusd. Preserve the preferred-exclusion-unproven flag.
2. For one common class, independently reconcile cover outstanding units to accepted sharesbas. Provider Primary Class is allowed when this evidence exists; a canonical active count of one alone is insufficient.
3. For ADS, bind issuer, security, ordinary classes, official ratio and effective dates. Calculate ordinary shares × exact reciprocal ordinary-per-ADS. Validate against provider cap within bounded price/factor precision; retain both declared factor and exact reviewed factor. Never reconstruct a missing/zero ratio from cap or pb. SCNI is admissible only through its official action evidence.
4. Apply direct-common identity overrides through reviewed temporal issuer/security evidence; do not silently relabel provider metadata. Class/perimeter and share freshness gates still apply.
5. Where several classes trade, use sum of class-specific prices × compatible class shares, or explicitly report a primary-price proxy as a separate metric. Prove full economic equivalence before aggregating an unlisted class at one quoted price. Voting differences, dividend parity on one date and one active canonical ticker are insufficient.
6. Record share-count date separately from filing availability. Check a scoped action/issuance/repurchase feed through current price date. Known changes without a reconciled settled count remain held. Establish how provider split adjustments are dated to prevent duplicate conversion. Carry-forward within a declared maximum age remains an approximation even when no event is known.
7. Native provider pb/cap/price/factor must remain immutable corroborating witnesses. Current numerator is price × validated economic units, divided by accepted positive parent equity; no Provider P/B, 4Q history, score, UI or accepted-quarter changes belong in this phase.

Classification totals are diagnostic, not deployment authorization. **SAFE** is 16; ADR/multi-class/identity generic-contract categories total **35**, of which only 15 additional rows beyond SAFE are currently basis-supported. Newer-count cases, unreviewed cases and the four actual price contradictions still need separate resolution. All **378** outside D remain held under the next evidence scope; the literal MUST_REMAIN_HELD classification is four. Unknown does not mean fundamentally unsafe, nor does internally consistent mean safe.
