# P/B.13 — Controlled publication of approved ownership reviews

Implementation and isolated-copy validation only, 2026-10-08. Baseline implementation: `92e4f94f94ec50167ed67243fe694ec5a439b08c`. The real active generation remains `pb_quarterly_ownership_v2_20261008T080025Z`; no real Production activation, role mutation, registry repin, scheduler change or push is performed.

This adds the missing operator publication step after [P/B.12 ownership approval](fundamentals_v4_pb_new_quarter_review_workflow.md), through the same immutable-generation and recovery contract used by [P/B.11](fundamentals_v4_pb_quarterly_ownership_v2_production_rollout.md). Ownership approval and Production publication remain separate decisions.

## Eligibility and selection

Only exact `PB_OWNERSHIP_NEW_QUARTER` cases whose lifecycle is `APPROVED` qualify. The selected case must still have its original approved V2 artifact and embedded canonical evidence. OPEN, HELD, resolved, unrelated review types and previously unsupported ownership scopes cannot be published. This action neither approves evidence nor changes publication authority or financial acceptance.

The bounded Admin action publishes **one explicitly selected approved case at a time**. The underlying workflow also supports an explicit list of approved case IDs, with independent validation and deterministic merging. There is no approve-all or publish-all action. An invalid selection fails the entire bundle; partial publication semantics are not introduced.

## Admin workflow

1. In the existing Fundamentals Admin Review Queue, refresh the quarterly P/B ownership section. It shows the count of approved pending publications, ticker/quarter, abbreviated observation/hash, reviewing operator/time, classification, approval artifact/source canonical hashes, current source generation and live publishability result.
2. Choose **Publish approved P/B ownership reviews** on the desired APPROVED case. This is distinct from **Approve quarterly ownership continuation**.
3. The workflow acquires existing Production/scheduler locks followed by the taxonomy lock, runs the existing recovery guard, and builds a fresh inactive three-role candidate from the current active generation.
4. Inspect the publication preview: exact selection; current and proposed generation IDs; current and candidate P/B statuses/values; active and candidate artifact SHA; all candidate role hashes; score-invariance and source-revalidation status; and the expected `APPROVED → PUBLISHED` transition.
5. Enter the publishing operator and explicitly check the separate Production publication confirmation. The Production button remains disabled until both are supplied. The backend independently requires literal `True`, a nonempty operator and explicit Production intent.
6. On confirmation the workflow reacquires the same locks, revalidates the exact saved preview and selected approvals, creates verified rollback backups and uses the existing durable journal and atomic generation-pointer activation. Queue consumption follows successful postflight only.

Preview does not activate a generation or change approval/financial evidence. It can reconcile an earlier completed publication's pending queue bookkeeping, or invoke the existing recovery guard for an incomplete earlier operation. Recovery that restores OLD requires a fresh invocation before continuing. There is no automatic publication after review approval or during normal refresh.

## Exact revalidation and current-generation rebase

The publication source is always resolved afresh through `resolve_active_generation(require_generation=True)`. It must have the complete three-role immutable layout, no role sidecars and hashes matching the active manifest. The workflow binds the active manifest/generation, complete role hashes, market/taxonomy main and WAL hashes, scheduler configuration and the relevant source-code/immutable V1/V2 files.

Each selected case is independently checked for:

- correct type and APPROVED lifecycle; exact case/candidate and approval hashes;
- same company/key/security/ticker and active-security validity;
- same latest accepted fiscal quarter/report period and observation/hash;
- same accepted sharesbas, raw factor, category, provider/availability dates, parent equity and accepted-share provenance;
- an unchanged prior official review still present in active history;
- the exact record originally generated by the P/B.12 approval operation, including operator/time/note and retained official evidence;
- valid exported artifact SHA and matching embedded original candidate artifact/pin containing that record;
- absence of a conflicting/already-active binding for the approved observation.

The prior approval's source generation may have changed. That alone does not invalidate a still-authoritative observation. The exact basis and prior history must remain compatible. A newer quarter, revised hash, changed count/factor/category/security/equity, altered evidence or changed review fails closed and requires new review/revalidation. Q3 approval cannot authorize Q4 shares.

The old P/B.12 canonical is read only to verify its evidence. Its financial contents are **never used** as publication roles. New provider and analysis candidates are byte-for-byte copies of the **current** active roles. New canonical starts from the **current** active canonical, with only the generation-local ownership artifact/configuration updated. This preserves unrelated intervening refreshes and score state without rebuilding scores.

## Deterministic merge and invariance

Start from the current active embedded artifact, falling back to the immutable bundled V2 registry for older generations. Retain all active historical records exactly in their existing order; append only explicitly selected approved records, sorted by their complete identity/observation/quarter binding. Reject duplicate or conflicting bindings and unsupported identity scopes. Never import other records from an old approval's combined artifact merely because that artifact happens to contain them.

The new artifact uses the existing deterministic V2 serialization and SHA binding in `v4_pb_ownership_artifact` and `v4_pb_reporting_contract`. The bundled `ownership_reviews_v2.json` and V1 remain unchanged. A repeated unchanged preview reuses its validated cached candidate and deterministic artifact; failed or moved candidates are rebuilt into a fresh attempt/generation.

Candidate validation and confirmation revalidation check:

- SQLite quick and foreign-key checks for all roles;
- exact provider and analysis SHA equality, covering all stored scoring, ranking, fingerprints/history and valuation state;
- ordered-row digests for every original canonical table and its schema, excluding only the two ownership artifact/config tables, covering financial statements, parent equity, provenance and publication authority;
- Provider P/B and four-quarter history equality for each selected company;
- active versus candidate Current P/B, approved record hash, new-Q economic units, new parent equity and unchanged formula.

Ordinary units are new accepted sharesbas. ADR units use new accepted sharesbas times the exact approved official rational factor, including the zero raw provider-factor case. No old share fallback, ratio inference or availability override is introduced. Independent price/equity/other hard gates may still hold the result; the preview reports the reason explicitly, and valid evidence can be published without forcing P/B availability.

## Existing durable publication path and final gate

`OwnershipPublicationWorkflow` is a bounded operation orchestrator; it uses the existing publication implementation rather than a separate rollback or publisher system:

1. Existing `production_lock` (Administration plus scheduler) followed by `taxonomy_operation_lock_context` and `guard_production_writes`.
2. Existing `_verified_backups(..., immutable_generation=True)` and `_candidate_manifest` for a complete verified OLD set.
3. Existing `prepare_journal(publication_mode='GENERATION_POINTER')`, with exact selected scope, approval/record hashes, publishing operator and proposed generation/artifact.
4. Existing `prepare_generation_from_candidates` and durable READY journal transition.
5. Immediately before activation, recheck active generation/manifest/role hashes, selected cases and approval artifacts, all input/code bindings, stable prepared candidate hashes, frozen market evidence and the exact owned journal transition while locks and a queue write transaction are held. Drift stops publication; candidates are not patched after this gate.
6. Existing `activate_prepared_generation` performs the atomic complete-generation pointer switch and fsynced journal transitions.
7. Postflight verifies active generation, complete role hashes/checks, financial digests, embedded artifact/pin, selected reports and unchanged input/code bindings.
8. Only after postflight passes, complete the existing durable journal and record the exact selected queue transitions in one transaction.

No active role is written in place. Real Production refuses overridden run/guard paths. Rehearsals require an explicitly isolated project and explicit rehearsal intent; they cannot point to actual live role files. Rehearsal taxonomy lock evidence stays under the repository's existing approved temp root, with isolated identifiers.

## Queue metadata, recovery and retries

Publication uses the existing ownership child table and shared queue audit log; there is no second publication queue. Each published case records generation ID, artifact SHA, publication timestamp, source candidate hash, approval hash, publishing operator and publication run reference. Unselected ownership cases and provider/publication-authority queue cases are untouched.

| Event | Active generation | Selected queue state |
|---|---|---|
| Preview/preparation only | OLD | APPROVED |
| Revalidation/pre-activation failure | OLD | APPROVED |
| Activation then failed postflight | Existing recovery restores/verifies OLD | APPROVED |
| Crash with incomplete journal | Existing recovery restores/verifies OLD; fresh retry required | APPROVED |
| Activation plus passed postflight | NEW | Exact selected cases PUBLISHED |
| Completed journal but interrupted queue commit | NEW with durable success receipt | APPROVED until idempotent receipt synchronization |

The completed/postflight-passed journal is the authority for queue consumption. `synchronize` requires the exact active generation and role/artifact hashes and consumes only the selected case/approval/record bindings in that receipt. An inactive candidate or an activation still awaiting postflight cannot consume approvals. This tightens P/B.12's former record-plus-active-path inference to require successful postflight evidence.

A durable completed receipt is also archived beside the saved preview. If final queue bookkeeping fails after successful activation/postflight, the error explicitly states that the generation was published and queue synchronization is required; it does not claim a rollback. The next publication invocation reconciles the completed receipt before proceeding. Normal Refresh Production also reconciles a completed P/B publication immediately after its existing recovery guard, before it can replace that journal with a later operation. Synchronization is transactional and creates no duplicate PUBLISHED audit events.

Repeated successful publication reports `ALREADY_PUBLISHED`, including when another selected review has since been published. A pre-activation failure or restore-OLD rollback allows a newly validated preview/retry; a recovered incomplete operation requires the existing fresh-invocation retry. Normal refresh can detect/preserve/reconcile queue cases, but never imports or publishes pending approvals automatically.

## Sequential publications

For A and B independently approved against an earlier generation:

1. Preview/publish A only. The merged artifact includes A and active history. B remains byte/semantically unchanged in APPROVED state.
2. Preview B against the now-active generation containing A. Revalidate B's unchanged accepted observation and original exact evidence.
3. Append B to the active history containing A, and copy the current provider/canonical/analysis state into a fresh complete generation.
4. Publish/postflight B, then consume B only. A's record/history/publication metadata remain preserved.

A stale Q3 case fails revalidation when Q4 is accepted, and normal reconciliation creates the distinct Q4 review case. Neither the stale canonical candidate nor Q3 share count can enter the Q4 calculation.
